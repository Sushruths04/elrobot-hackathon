ARCHITECTURE

How It Works — Local Closed-Loop Pipeline

  • Fresh RGB Grounding: Object poses are measured right before motion via calibrated height planes (4, 6, 10 cm) — never hardcoded.
  • Right Tool Mechanics: Horizontal lip strokes open hinged caps; vertical pulls extract soft wipes; the rigid packet edge sweeps loose beans.
  • Honest Visual Checks: Thin wipes do not stall servo current, so before/after camera checks verify lid opening, sheet hold, and clean tables.
01 • AUTONOMOUS DESK ORGANISATION

Step 1: Autonomous Pen & Marker Pick-and-Place

Autonomous camera + 7-DoF IK runs (~23 s cycle; motor-8 stall ticks distinguish thin blue pens at 1397 ticks from thick markers at 1570 ticks).

02 • CENTER-MAGNET BOTTLE-CAP PICKUP

Step 2: Center-Magnet Duster Bottle-Cap Cleanup

Because the magnet sits only in the dead center of the duster, the robot aligns that center 1.2 cm above each cap so they snap up without pressing, verifies 0 caps remain, and bins them.

03 • HERO DEFORMABLE MANIPULATION

Step 3: Chained Flip-Top Lid Open & Tissue Extraction

A single horizontal stroke at z = 10 cm catches the flip-top lip and pops the cap wide open; the gripper immediately pinches the standing fold at z = 7.6 cm and pulls one tissue straight out.

04 • EMERGENT TOOL ADAPTATION &amp; BEAN TRANSFER

Step 4: Spill Adaptation — 5-Lane Tissue Wipe vs. Smart Box Push & Spoon Scoop

First it wiped spilled beans in a 5-lane tissue pattern; then it adapted intelligently — pushing the rigid packet box sideways as a 21 cm squeegee! Plus dual-camera 30 fps bowl-to-cup spoon scooping.

05 • DIGITAL TWIN &amp; 30 HZ REINFORCEMENT LEARNING

Step 5: Side-by-Side Digital Twin & 30 Hz Table-Tennis RL

Side-by-side MuJoCo 3.2.7 twin (6.79 mm fit, 94% cup benchmark) and our main dynamic goal: 30 Hz RL racket ball balancing (95% in twin; real 12 V servos lacked holding torque for the outstretched bat).
